How it works

One loop. Seven steps. Two kinds of owner.

AI runs the experiments and remembers every result. Your team approves what runs and decides what changes.

  1. Goal, human: your team sets the outcome and the limits.
  2. Study, AI: AI studies how the workflow really runs today.
  3. Propose, AI: AI proposes a specific, testable change to the human–AI split.
  4. Approve, human: a person approves, adjusts or declines.
  5. Run, AI: the experiment runs on live work against a fair holdout.
  6. Doubt, AI: the Skeptic attacks the result before anyone trusts it.
  7. Remember, AI: every result, failures included, goes into the evidence ledger.

The product

Instruments for a half-human, half-AI company.

Model-switch lab

Test a new AI model against your current one, or against your team, on the same live work before you switch.

Failure memory

Every experiment that didn’t work is kept with the reason, so your team never repeats a failed test.

Supervision designer

Decide who drafts, who reviews and who approves in each workflow, then test the design before you roll it out.

Principles

Our principles

01

Evidence over hype

The AI industry runs on bold claims. We run on experiments, and when something isn’t proven, we say so.

02

Humans in command

AI should handle the routine, not make the calls. People set the goals, approve every change and can overrule any result.

03

Wellbeing is a metric

A workflow that saves money but burns out the team isn’t a win. Workload and quality are measured alongside cost.
